# Zoom lifecycle

[`ZoomFeature`](../../../src/UI-Tweaks/UI-Tweaks/ModFeatures/ZoomFeature.cs) loads on every
client session. It owns the zoom hotkey, Zoom renderer, rendering resources, and
Zoom Harmony patches. Each config notification checks the current `Zoom.Enable`
value and registers or removes the hotkey. Disabling also clears any held Zoom
request before removing the hotkey.

## Zoom renderer activation

The Zoom renderer is not registered at startup. A Zoom hotkey event registers it,
and it remains registered while zooming in, fully zoomed-in, and returning to the
normal view.

Zoom becomes stable only after the hotkey is released and progress returns to `0`.
It then queues removal of the Zoom renderer outside the current render callback,
rechecking stability before removal in case Zoom was activated again.

## Cleanup

Disposal clears the Zoom hotkey handler, unregisters the Zoom renderer, releases
rendering resources and patches, and restores shared field-of-view and
mouse-sensitivity factors to `1`. Queued Zoom renderer removal becomes a no-op
after disposal.
